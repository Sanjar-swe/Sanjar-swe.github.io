How it works

Three steps, about ninety seconds

  1. 1

    Pick a question

    Real Part 1, Part 2 cue-card and Part 3 discussion questions, generated across the topics the exam actually uses.

  2. 2

    Speak or type

    Tap the mic and answer out loud, or type it if you are somewhere you cannot speak. Both get scored.

  3. 3

    Read the feedback

    A band estimate per criterion, the specific errors that cost you marks, and what a stronger answer sounds like.

A worked example

What one evaluation actually tells you

Not a grade with no explanation attached. A number per criterion, the reason behind it, and one thing to change next time.

Part 2 · Cue card

“Describe a habit you have that you think is good. Explain why you started it and how it affects your life.”

7.0
Band estimate
Fluency & Coherence7.0

You keep going without long pauses and your ideas follow each other cleanly. The hesitation shows up when you reach for an example, not when you run out of language.

Lexical Resource6.5

Accurate but safe. "Good", "important" and "a lot of" carry too much of the answer — this is the criterion holding your overall band down.

Grammatical Range & Accuracy7.5

A real mix of structures, including two conditionals used naturally. The article slips are minor and never obscure your meaning.

Pronunciationfrom audio7.0

Clear and easy to follow throughout. Word stress on longer words is where an examiner would still hear effort.

Key improvement

Replace three general adjectives with precise ones. “It has a good effect on my life” becomes “it has made me noticeably more disciplined” — same idea, and the band moves on Lexical Resource alone.

Premium reads the band 8 version aloud so you can hear the rhythm, not just read it.
